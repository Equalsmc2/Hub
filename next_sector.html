1. Abstract & Environmental Origins

Ichor is a naturally occurring, highly anomalous liquefied metal restricted entirely to zones of extreme thermal output. Geological surveys indicate that the substance forms exclusively on celestial bodies locked in extreme proximity to stellar entities or planetary cores boasting hyper-thermal thermodynamic baselines. It remains strictly in a liquid state, defying expected vaporization thresholds at these temperatures.

3. Medical Application & Quasi-Biological Autonomy

The primary sanctioned application for Ichor is advanced medical trauma response. When applied to severely damaged biological tissue, the liquid metal acts as an unparalleled regenerative catalyst, capable of instantaneously binding and healing catastrophic wounds at the cellular level.

Most notably, Ichor exhibits undocumented behaviors typically associated with living organisms. In uncontained environments, the substance displays self-directed motility, shifting and traversing its surroundings on its own accord. This quasi-biological autonomy suggests a primitive, systemic reactivity that is not yet fully understood by our xenobiology teams, necessitating absolute containment when integrated into standard Trauma Suit medical reservoirs.
